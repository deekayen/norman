Donate

Norman is free, and it's staying that way. If it saved your fingers some work, you can say thanks with a donation through whichever service you like:

  • GitHub Sponsors, through your GitHub account
  • Ko-fi
  • Liberapay, for recurring donations
  • PayPal
  • Venmo (US only)
  • Buy Me a Coffee

Each service handles the payment itself, so I never see your card or bank details.

Can't donate? Blogging, posting, or telling a friend about Norman helps too.
